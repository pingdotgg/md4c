
# Escaped Text

With the flag `ENRMRKD_FLAG_ESCAPEDTEXT`, MD4C reports a backslash-escaped
character in normal text as `ENRMRKD_TEXT_ESCAPED`. Its text is the character
without the backslash, so the HTML renderer output is unchanged.

```````````````````````````````` example
C:\Users\me\.t3\_build\shot.png and \*not emphasis\*
.
<p>C:\Users\me.t3_build\shot.png and *not emphasis*</p>
.
--fescaped-text
````````````````````````````````

Escapes keep their meaning in code spans, link destinations and titles.

```````````````````````````````` example
`a\.b` [x\.y](/p\.q "t\.u")
.
<p><code>a\.b</code> <a href="/p.q" title="t.u">x.y</a></p>
.
--fescaped-text
````````````````````````````````

A backslash before a newline is still a hard break.

```````````````````````````````` example
foo\
bar
.
<p>foo<br>
bar</p>
.
--fescaped-text
````````````````````````````````
